Hospital Management System

User Manual

A screen-by-screen guide to every module in the system — how to log in, what each page is for, and how to add or update records. Screenshots below were captured while signed in as an Administrator, so a few management-only screens (like Role & Permission Management) may not be visible to every role.

Getting Started

Open the application in your browser and sign in with the email and password given to you by your hospital administrator. New patients can also self-register from the login screen using "Create an account."

What you see after signing in depends on your role — Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, or Patient. Each role only sees the modules it has permission to use; an Administrator can change this from Role & Permission Management.

AdminFull access to every module, including settings and role permissions.
DoctorPatients, appointments, medical records, prescriptions, vitals, lab/radiology orders.
NurseVitals, medication chart (MAR), OPD queue, ward/bed support.
ReceptionistPatient registration, appointments, admissions, billing, OPD tokens.
PatientTheir own profile, appointments, bills, records, and the AI Assistant.
1

Dashboard

/dashboard

The home page after signing in. Shows quick counts (patients, doctors, appointments, pending lab reports), a monthly revenue chart, and analytics tiles such as active patients, no-show rate, and cancellation rate. Use this page to get a quick pulse of the hospital's activity for the day.

2

Patients

/patients

To add a patient: click Add Patient, fill in first/last name, date of birth, gender, phone, and optionally email, blood group, address, and emergency contact, then click Create patient. Click a patient's name in the list to open their full profile, edit details, or deactivate them.

3

Doctors

/doctors

To add a doctor: click Add Doctor and enter their name, specialization, department, license number, years of experience, and consultation fee. Doctors created here also get a login account so they can sign in to the system.

4

Departments

/departments

Manage the hospital's clinical departments (e.g. Cardiology, General Medicine, Pediatrics). Departments are used when assigning doctors and when booking appointments or admissions. Click Add Department to create a new one.

5

Appointments

/appointments

To book an appointment: click Book Appointment, search for and select the patient and doctor, then pick an available date and time slot. From the list you can also reschedule, cancel, or update the status of an existing appointment. A reminder is sent to the patient automatically before the appointment.

6

Admissions

/admissions

To admit a patient: click Admit Patient, choose the patient, ward, and bed, and enter the admitting doctor and reason. From the list you can later transfer the patient to a different bed or discharge them, which frees up the bed automatically.

7

Wards

/wards

Manage wards (e.g. General Ward, ICU, Maternity) and the beds inside each one. Click Add Ward to create a new ward, then open a ward to add its individual beds. Bed status (Available/Occupied/Maintenance) updates automatically as patients are admitted or discharged.

8

Medical Records

/medical-records

Stores each patient's diagnosis and visit notes. New records are written by the treating Doctor against a specific appointment — Administrators and front-desk staff can view this list, but only clinical staff can add or edit an entry, since it contains clinical notes.

9

Vitals

/vitals

To record vitals: click Record Vitals, pick the patient, and enter blood pressure, temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, and SpO2. If any reading crosses a critical threshold, the system automatically raises a Critical Vital alert visible on the Alerts page and the header notification bell.

10

Prescriptions

/prescriptions

Doctors prescribe one or more medicines to a patient here, each with its own dosage, frequency, and duration. The prescription can then be printed or downloaded, and its items feed directly into the Medication Chart for nurses to administer.

11

Medication Chart (MAR)

/medication-administrations

To log a dose: click the add button, select the patient and the prescribed medicine, then record whether the dose was Administered or Skipped at a given date and time. A skipped dose automatically raises an alert so the care team is notified.

12

Lab Reports

/lab-reports

A lab test follows three steps: 1) click Order Test to order it for a patient (status starts as Pending); 2) once the sample is drawn, click the flask icon to Collect Sample and record the sample type; 3) once results are ready, click the pencil icon to enter the result and mark it Completed — ticking "Critical result" automatically raises an alert for the care team.

13

Radiology

/radiology

To order a scan: click Order Scan, choose the patient and the type of imaging needed (X-ray, CT, MRI, etc.). Once complete, the report and any findings can be recorded and shared with the ordering doctor.

14

OPD Queue

/opd-queue

Manages the walk-in token queue for a doctor's clinic. To issue a token, click Issue Token and select the patient and doctor. Staff at the doctor's console then use Call Next, Start, Complete, or Skip to move each token through the queue. A read-only, login-free waiting-room display of the live queue is available at /display/opd-queue — ideal for a TV screen in the waiting area.

15

OT Scheduling

/ot-scheduling

To schedule a surgery: click Schedule Surgery, pick the patient, surgeon, operation theatre, and the planned date/time. This checks the selected theatre's availability automatically to avoid double-booking.

16

Operation Theatres

/operation-theatres

Manage the hospital's physical operation theatre rooms that surgeries are scheduled against. Click Add Theatre to register a new OT room and its status.

17

Blood Bank

/blood-bank

Tracks blood donors, blood unit stock by blood group, and requests for blood from wards/departments. Use the tabs to register a donor, log a new blood unit into inventory, or raise a blood request. Units nearing expiry are flagged automatically.

18

Referrals

/referrals

Used when a doctor refers a patient onward to another doctor or department. Click New Referral, choose the patient, the referring and receiving doctor, and a reason. The receiving side can accept, decline, or complete the referral.

19

Assets

/assets

Inventory of hospital equipment (beds, monitors, machines, etc.) and their maintenance history. Click Add Asset to register new equipment, and log maintenance visits against it as they happen.

20

Patient Feedback

/patient-feedback

Patients can share feedback and a rating about their visit or care experience from here by clicking Share Feedback. Staff can review submitted feedback to spot recurring issues.

21

Emergency

/emergency

Logs walk-in emergency department visits separately from the regular appointment flow, including triage priority. Click the add button to register a new emergency visit as soon as a patient arrives.

22

Medicines

/medicines

The master catalogue of medicines the pharmacy stocks, including unit price and reorder level. Click Add Medicine to add a new medicine to the catalogue before it can be stocked or prescribed.

23

Pharmacy

/pharmacy

Day-to-day pharmacy stock operations: view batches with expiry dates, manually receive stock, dispense stock against a prescription, and see low-stock or expiring-soon medicines at a glance. For a full paper trail against a supplier, use Purchase Orders instead of manual receiving.

24

Vendors

/vendors

Manage the suppliers you buy medicines from. Click Add Vendor and enter their name, contact phone/email, and address — this is required before you can create a Purchase Order against them.

25

Purchase Orders

/purchase-orders

To order medicines from a supplier: click New Purchase Order, pick a vendor, and add one or more medicine lines with quantity and price. When stock physically arrives, open the order and click Receive Goods to record batch numbers and expiry dates — this automatically creates stock batches and updates medicine stock levels, and the order's status moves from Ordered → Partially Received → Received.

26

Staff Scheduling

/staff-scheduling

Plan shift/duty schedules for doctors and nurses. Click the add button to assign a staff member to a shift on a given date, so managers can see who is on duty at any time.

27

Consents

/consents

To request a consent form: click Request Consent, choose the patient and the procedure it covers. The patient (or their representative) can then review and grant or decline it, and the outcome is recorded for compliance.

28

Patient Documents

/patient-documents

Upload and store scanned documents against a patient's profile — ID proofs, previous reports, referral letters, and so on. Click the add button, choose the patient, and upload the file.

29

Billing

/billing

To create a bill: click New Bill, select the patient, and add one or more line items — each with a description, type, amount, quantity, an optional HSN/SAC code, and a GST rate (0/5/12/18/28%). The subtotal, tax, and total are calculated live as you type. Open a bill to record a payment, cancel it, or click Download PDF for a GST-compliant invoice showing the hospital's GSTIN and a CGST/SGST breakdown.

30

Insurance Providers

/insurance-providers

Master list of insurance companies the hospital works with (e.g. Star Health, HDFC Ergo). Click Add Provider before you can link a patient's policy to that insurer.

31

Insurance Policies

/insurance-policies

Links a patient to their insurance policy number, provider, and coverage validity dates. Click Add Policy and select the patient and provider — this is needed before filing an insurance claim or pre-authorization for that patient.

32

Insurance Claims

/claims

File a claim against an existing bill for a patient's insurance policy, track its approved amount, and settle it once the insurer pays out — the linked bill's paid amount updates automatically on settlement.

33

Insurance Pre-Authorizations

/insurance-pre-authorizations

Used to request an insurer's approval before an expensive procedure or admission, rather than claiming after the fact. Click Request Pre-Authorization, pick the patient's policy and the relevant admission, and submit the requested amount for the insurer to approve or reject.

34

AI Assistant

/ai-assistant

A chat assistant you can ask hospital-related questions in plain language — for example "What are my upcoming appointments?" or "Show me my last lab report." It only answers using information you're allowed to see: patients get answers about themselves, while staff get broader, role-appropriate access.

35

Audit Logs

/audit-logs

A read-only, system-wide trail of who changed what and when — visible to Administrators only. Use it to investigate a change or confirm an action was taken. There is nothing to add here; it fills in automatically as the system is used.

36

AI Interaction Logs

/ai-interaction-logs

A read-only oversight log of conversations users have had with the AI Assistant, visible to Administrators only — useful for reviewing AI behaviour and answer quality over time.

37

Hospital Settings

/hospital-settings

Admin-only, hospital-wide configuration: hospital name, address, contact details, working hours, the default appointment slot length, and the hospital's GSTIN (used on billing invoices). Update the fields and click Save changes.

38

Alerts

/alerts

A central feed of clinical alerts — critical vitals, critical lab results, skipped medication doses, and manually raised concerns. Clinical staff can Acknowledge and then Resolve an alert. Staff can also raise one manually via Raise Alert. The same live feed also appears under the bell icon in the top header, wherever you are in the app.

39

Role & Permission Management

/role-permissions

Admin-only screen to control exactly which modules each role (Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Patient) can View, Create, Edit, or Delete. Select a role tab, tick or untick the checkboxes per module, then click Save Changes. Affected users see the new permissions the next time they log in or their session refreshes.
